# Cat Care Basics

Source: https://www.avma.org/resources-tools/pet-owners/petcare/cat-care

Cats generally benefit from regular feeding, fresh water, a clean litter area, enrichment, and opportunities for restful, low-stress time. Indoor cats may need environmental activities that allow climbing, exploring, hiding, and gentle play. Avoid using products that are not labeled safe for cats.

Routine veterinary care helps address vaccination, parasite prevention, dental concerns, and changes in behavior or health. A sudden change in urination, appetite, grooming, energy, or litter-box use may need prompt professional attention.
