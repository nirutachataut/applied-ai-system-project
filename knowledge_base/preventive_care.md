# General Preventive Care

Source: https://www.avma.org/resources-tools/pet-owners/petcare

Preventive care is individual to the pet and commonly includes regular
veterinary examinations, appropriate vaccinations, parasite prevention,
dental care, and monitoring of appetite, body weight, behavior, and waste.
A veterinarian can recommend an appropriate preventive schedule based on age,
species, lifestyle, health history, and local disease risk.

Owners can support preventive care by keeping a current care routine,
recording unusual changes, and scheduling professional follow-up promptly.
Sudden illness, breathing difficulty, poisoning, severe pain, seizures,
trauma, or collapse requires urgent professional help rather than a routine
care suggestion.
