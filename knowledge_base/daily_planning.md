# Daily Pet-Care Planning

Source: https://www.humaneworld.org/en/resources/pet-care

A daily routine can organize feeding, water checks, exercise, enrichment, grooming, medication reminders, rest, and supervised time. The owner should consider the pet's species, age, health, temperament, household schedule, and available time. PawPal+'s scheduler is an organizational tool and should not override veterinary instructions or observed changes in the pet.

Plan realistic durations and leave room for rest and unexpected needs. Use scheduled times and conflict warnings to identify overlap, then review each task manually. Reassess the plan when a pet is sick, stressed, recovering, or otherwise unable to follow the usual routine.
