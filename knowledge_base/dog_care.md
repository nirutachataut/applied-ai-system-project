# Dog Care Basics

Source: https://www.avma.org/resources-tools/pet-owners/petcare/dog-care

Dogs need daily care that may include fresh water, species-appropriate food, physical activity, and supervision. A routine should reflect the dog's age, health, size, and veterinarian guidance. Sudden changes in appetite, thirst, activity, or waste can warrant professional advice.

Regular contact with a veterinarian is important for preventive care, vaccinations, parasite control, and dental health. Exercise needs vary substantially among breeds and individual dogs, so a dog owner should use a level of activity appropriate to the animal and seek advice when uncertainty exists.

## References
