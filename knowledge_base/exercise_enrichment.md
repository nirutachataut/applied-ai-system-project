# Exercise and Mental Enrichment

Source: https://www.rspcapetinsurance.org.au/pet-care/dogs-and-cats/exercise-and-mental-stimulation

Physical exercise and mental stimulation can be part of a balanced daily routine. Safe walking, supervised play, food puzzles, training games, climbing areas for cats, and rotating accessible toys can provide engagement. Activities should match the pet's age, mobility, health, and individual preferences.

Start gradually when increasing activity, provide water and rest, and stop if the pet appears distressed, overheated, unusually tired, or in pain. Do not assume that every dog needs the same walking duration or every cat needs the same play schedule.
