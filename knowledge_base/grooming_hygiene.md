# Grooming and Hygiene

Source: https://www.aspca.org/pet-care/cat-care/cat-grooming-tips

Grooming needs vary by species and coat. Brushing can reduce loose hair and help owners notice skin or coat changes, while nail care and dental care may require species-appropriate tools and professional guidance. Use pet-safe products and avoid forcing a fearful or painful pet through a procedure.

Clean the living area and litter or sleeping area regularly. Bathing should not be excessive or performed with human products unless a veterinarian recommends a specific product. Persistent itching, odor, skin injury, broken nails, or mouth problems warrant professional assessment.
