# Feeding and Hydration Basics

Source: https://www.avma.org/resources-tools/pet-owners/petcare

Feed pets a complete and balanced diet appropriate to their species and life stage. Follow label directions and any individualized guidance from a veterinarian rather than guessing from another animal or family member's routine. Fresh drinking water should be available unless a veterinarian gives different instructions.

Sudden dietary changes can upset digestion. Introduce changes gradually and ask a veterinarian before adding supplements or changing a medical diet. Toxic foods, choking hazards, and unsafe feeding practices should be avoided.
